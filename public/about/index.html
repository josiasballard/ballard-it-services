THE IDEA

Small businesses deserve someone reliable to call.

Small businesses rely on computers, Microsoft 365, employee accounts, shared files, printers, cloud services, and other technology every day — but many do not need or cannot justify hiring a full-time internal IT employee.

Ballard IT Services is built around that gap: provide dependable, understandable IT support without making routine technology harder than it needs to be.

Because the business is owner-operated, clients work directly with the person responsible for their support. There is no large help-desk queue or constant handoff between unfamiliar technicians.

The goal is straightforward: understand the problem, solve it properly, communicate clearly, document what matters, and help keep the business moving.

HOW SUPPORT WORKS

Start with the problem. Keep the solution practical.

01

Direct communication

Work directly with the person responsible for handling the issue instead of being passed through multiple layers of support.

02

Remote first

Most problems begin with remote troubleshooting so support can start quickly and unnecessary travel can be avoided.

03

Local when needed

When a problem requires hands-on attention, onsite support is available in Dover and surrounding Central Delaware.

04

Clear boundaries

Work is scoped honestly. Specialized problems that require deeper expertise are identified rather than treated as something that should be guessed through.
